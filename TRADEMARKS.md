# Trademark policy

"sos-vault", the sos-vault wordmark, and the sos-vault logo (the green safe-door icon) are trademarks of the sos-vault project owner.

## What you can do without asking

- Refer to the project by name in articles, blog posts, talks, and other commentary.
- Use the logo in unmodified form when linking to the project.
- Describe your own software as "built with sos-vault" or "compatible with sos-vault" when it actually is.

## What requires permission

- Distributing a modified fork under the same name. Please rename your fork (for example, "sos-vault-fork" is fine; "sos-vault Pro" is not).
- Selling a service whose name includes "sos-vault" or whose branding is confusable with ours.
- Using the logo in marketing for a product that competes with sos-vault.
- Producing physical merchandise (stickers, shirts, etc.) bearing the wordmark or logo for resale.

For permission, email `trademarks@sos-vault.com` with a description of the intended use.

## What we ask

The AGPLv3 license grants you broad rights to use, modify, and redistribute the source code. The trademark policy is a thin overlay on top of that license: keep the project's identity clean so users can find the canonical source and so the "open core" reputation we are trying to build is not undermined by lookalike products.

If you fork sos-vault and distribute the result, rename your fork and remove our logo from the product chrome. You are welcome to credit "based on sos-vault" anywhere it is accurate.
